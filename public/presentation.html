🎯 Project Motivation

Objective & Problem Statement

Bridging the gap between raw edge sensors and verifiable, AI-assisted electrical intelligence

⚠️ Problem Statement

  • Delayed / Post-Facto Data: Conventional energy meters provide monthly totals without real-time, load-level instantaneous power visibility.
  • Critical Motor Fault Blindness: DC/AC pump motors experience 1.5–2.5× startup surges and stall currents (>2.5A) that cause coil burnout without detection.
  • AI Math Hallucinations in IoT: Unconstrained LLMs frequently guess sensor values, confuse instantaneous Watts with accumulated kWh, or invent invalid bills.
  • Edge Calibration Skew: Uncalibrated sensor dividers and electrical noise distort telemetry if left unverified by a single source of truth.

🎯 Project Objectives

  • High-Speed Dual-Load Telemetry: Continuously sample voltage and current from an LED fixture and 5V DC Motor at 1 Hz via ESP32 ADC.
  • Deterministic Backend Engine: Execute strict mathematical DC formulas ($P = V \times I$) and timestamp-based trapezoidal integration ($\Delta E$) in Node.js.
  • Cloud Aggregation & Persistence: Stream 1-minute statistical aggregates (Min/Max/Avg) to Firebase RTDB and persist accumulators across restarts.
  • Autonomous Groq AI Copilot: Feed verified JSON to Groq (`openai/gpt-oss-120b`) for fault diagnosis, surge alerts, and Indian Rupee (₹8.00/kWh) projections.

⚡ Critical Electrical Safety Rules

  • ESP32 ADC 3.3V Overvoltage Protection: The ACS712 operates at 5V $V_{CC}$ and outputs 2.5V at zero current, reaching up to 4.5V under load. Connecting this directly to an ESP32 ADC pin would damage the MCU. A matched $10\text{k}\Omega/10\text{k}\Omega$ voltage divider scales the sensor output to $\le 2.5\text{V}$, safely below the 3.3V threshold.
  • 1N4007 Inductive Flyback Clamping: The DC motor generates negative inductive back-EMF spikes during de-energization. A 1N4007 reverse-biased diode is wired across motor terminals to absorb transient kickback.
  • Star Common Ground: All sensor grounds, the 5V DC bench power supply, and ESP32 GND share a unified reference to eliminate floating ADC errors.

🚀 Summary & Roadmap

Conclusion & Future Scope

Achievements realized and prospective enhancements for industrial deployment

✅ Project Accomplishments

  • End-to-End IoT Pipeline: Successfully engineered an edge-to-cloud facility monitoring platform connecting ESP32 to Firebase RTDB and Groq AI.
  • Eliminated Math Inconsistencies: Solved LLM hallucinations by centralizing calculations in a backend JSON data contract.
  • High-Density UI: Designed a zero-scroll responsive web app with 100vh desktop fit, mobile tab switching, and locked bottom chat bar.
  • Physical Fault Awareness: Validated classification for DC motor startup surge, nominal operation, and stall scenarios.

🔮 Future Scope & Industrialization

  • 3-Phase AC Grid Monitoring: Integrate non-invasive split-core Current Transformers (SCT-013 100A) and PZEM-004T for 230V/415V utility monitoring.
  • Active Solid-State Relay Tripping: Implement hardware relay interlocking to auto-trip loads upon detecting persistent motor stall (>2.5A for >3s).
  • TinyML Edge Intelligence: Deploy on-device TensorFlow Lite Micro neural networks on ESP32 for offline anomaly detection without cloud dependence.
  • KSEB Time-of-Day (ToD) Optimization: Automated load-shedding scheduling during peak tariff windows (6:00 PM – 10:00 PM).
