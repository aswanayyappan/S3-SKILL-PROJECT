🎯 Project Motivation

Objective & Problem Statement

Bridging the gap between raw edge sensors and verifiable, AI-assisted electrical intelligence

⚠️ Problem Statement

  • Passive / Delayed Utility Data: Conventional smart meters provide post-facto monthly billing without instantaneous load-level breakdown.
  • Critical Motor Fault Blindness: DC/AC inductive motors undergo extreme startup surges (1.5–2.5× nominal) and stall conditions (>2.5A) that cause thermal failure without real-time detection.
  • AI Math Hallucinations in IoT: Off-the-shelf LLMs frequently invent sensor readings, confuse cumulative energy with instant power, or miscalculate tiered utility bills (e.g. KSEB slabs).
  • Calibration Discrepancies: Sensor noise and uncalibrated divider ratios skew physical telemetry if left unvalidated by an edge-cloud mathematical contract.

🎯 Project Objectives

  • High-Speed Dual-Load Telemetry: Continuously sample DC LED fixture and 5V DC Motor current and voltage at 1 Hz using ESP32 ADC.
  • Deterministic Single Source of Truth: Execute strict mathematical DC formulas ($P = V \times I$) and timestamp-based trapezoidal integration ($\Delta E = \frac{P_1 + P_2}{2} \times \Delta t$) in a Node.js backend.
  • Cloud Aggregation & Persistence: Stream 1-minute statistical aggregates (Min/Max/Avg) to Firebase RTDB and persist accumulator state across restarts.
  • Autonomous AI Energy Copilot: Feed structured JSON to Groq AI (`openai/gpt-oss-120b`) for zero-hallucination energy auditing and Indian Rupee (₹8.00/kWh) projections.

🚀 Summary & Roadmap

Conclusion & Future Scope

Achievements realized and prospective enhancements for industrial deployment

✅ Project Accomplishments

  • End-to-End IoT Pipeline: Successfully engineered an edge-to-cloud facility monitoring platform connecting ESP32 to Firebase RTDB and Groq AI.
  • Eliminated Math Inconsistencies: Solved LLM hallucinations by centralizing calculations in a backend JSON data contract.
  • High-Density UI: Designed a zero-scroll responsive web app with 100vh desktop fit, mobile tab switching, and locked bottom chat bar.
  • Physical Fault Awareness: Validated classification for DC motor startup surge, nominal operation, and stall scenarios.

🔮 Future Scope & Industrialization

  • 3-Phase AC Grid Monitoring: Integrate non-invasive split-core Current Transformers (SCT-013) and PZEM-004T for 230V/415V utility monitoring.
  • Active Solid-State Relay Tripping: Implement hardware relay interlocking to auto-trip loads upon detecting persistent motor stall (>2.5A for >3s).
  • TinyML Edge Intelligence: Deploy on-device TensorFlow Lite Micro neural networks on ESP32 for offline anomaly detection without cloud dependence.
  • KSEB Time-of-Day (ToD) Optimization: Automated load-shedding scheduling during peak tariff windows (6:00 PM – 10:00 PM).
