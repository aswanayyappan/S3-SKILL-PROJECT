Problem Formulation

Objectives & Technical Problem Statement

  • Clean Solar Energy Harvesting: Buffer power from a 6V Solar PV Panel (5–10W) into a 3.7V Li-ion battery via TP4056 and step-up to stable 5V DC via booster.
  • Dual 5V Load Monitoring: Provide discrete telemetry for two distinct 5V loads (DC LED fixture on GPIO 32/34 and 5V DC Motor on GPIO 33/35).
  • Sensor Zero-Bias Clamping: Raw physical sensors float at ~1.18V and ~0.2A when turned OFF, accumulating false phantom kWh. Our digital deadband clamps inactive lines to true 0.00V / 0.00A.
  • PyTorch Neural Transformer: Trained multi-head self-attention network achieving 97.22% accuracy in classifying electrical operations and detecting motor stalls.
  • Autonomous Continuous Learning: Background learning daemon fine-tunes model weights every 5 minutes from live Firebase telemetry via experience replay buffer.
  • Official Model Context Protocol (MCP): Direct standardized tools interface for ChatGPT Custom GPT Actions and Claude Desktop.

Deadband Calibration Impact

  • Without Deadband: Unpowered lines read ~1.18V & ~0.2A, accumulating 0.283W phantom power per socket (~₹1.63/month false charge).
  • With Zero-Bias Deadband: Inactive lines clamped strictly to 0.00V and 0.000A, guaranteeing zero billing distortion.
  • Tariff Precision: Accumulation of 0.0987 kWh at ₹8.00/kWh correctly yields exact ₹0.79 energy charge.
